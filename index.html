Airport Shuttle Service in Grand Rapids: A Simple Guide to Getting To and From Ford Airport

Getting to the airport should be the easy part of a trip. Here is a plain, friendly look at your choices, so you can pick the ride that fits your day.

Why More People Need a Good Ride to the Airport

More people than ever fly out of West Michigan. According to the Gerald R. Ford International Airport, 4,303,696 guests flew through the airport in 2025. That broke the old record. The airport first passed four million passengers in 2024.

The same announcement says the airport is the second-busiest in Michigan. It offers nonstop flights to more than 35 places and supports over 40,000 jobs in the region.

More passengers mean more cars, more bags, and more people trying to get to the same curb. That is why picking your ride early is a smart move. An airport shuttle service in Grand Rapids is one of several ways to do it, and it helps to know how each one works.

Your Ground Transportation Choices at GRR

The airport says it has a ground transportation option for every budget, from car rentals to limousines. Its ground transportation page lists the main choices below.

Rideshare

Uber and Lyft both work in Grand Rapids and at the airport. You use the app on your phone. To find your ride, go out through door 4, across from the Delta ticket counter, to the Rideshare Shelter. This is the one spot where all rideshare cars pick up and drop off.

Taxi

To find a taxi, leave the terminal through door 5 or door 7 and walk to the Parking Shuttle Shelter. A taxi starter there helps you get a cab. You can also book a cab online ahead of time.

The Rapid Bus

The Rapid bus system runs Route 27 to the airport on weekdays, Monday through Friday. A ride costs $1.75. This is the lowest-cost choice, but it does not run on weekends, and it may not go near your final stop.

Hotel Shuttles

Many hotels near the airport run free rides for guests. Check the transportation information board in the baggage claim area to see which hotels offer this. Pickup is at the shelter across from short-term parking, near door 4.

Rental Cars

Car rental counters now sit in a new rental car facility at 5630 Gateway Dr SE. The airport asks drivers to follow the blue line on its map to return cars, which avoids terminal traffic.

Pre-Booked Private Shuttle or Car

This is a ride you book before your trip. A driver is set up for your flight and your address. You do not have to open an app, hunt for a shelter, or wait for a cab to show up.

When an Airport Shuttle Makes the Most Sense

No single ride is best for everyone. Here is when a booked shuttle is often the calmer pick:

  • You have a lot of bags. Families and groups often need more room than a small car gives.
  • You have an early flight. A ride that is already set up means one less thing to worry about at 4 a.m.
  • You are traveling in a group. One vehicle for everyone is easier than two or three separate cars.
  • You are going far. Trips to places like Holland or Grand Haven cost more by cab, so a known price helps.
  • You want to skip the guesswork. A planned pickup takes away the wait for a car that may or may not come.

If these sound like you, look into a pre-booked airport shuttle service in Grand Rapids. When you book, share your airline, flight number, number of people, and bags. That way the right size of vehicle is ready for you.

Think of it like this: A bus is like a train that goes to the same places every day. A taxi is a ride you wave down. A booked shuttle is a ride that is waiting for you.

How to Plan Your Ride Step by Step

A little planning goes a long way. Try these steps:

  1. Count your people and bags. This tells you how big the vehicle needs to be.
  2. Work backward from your flight. The airport suggests you reach the ticket counter no less than two hours before takeoff. It also says airlines want you at the gate at least 45 minutes before departure. Add time for the drive and for security.
  3. Pick your ride early. The busiest times, like holidays and summer, fill up fast.
  4. Save the pickup spot. Know which door and shelter you will use when you land.
  5. Have a backup. Keep a second option in mind in case a flight is late or the weather turns bad.

These timing tips come from the airport’s own travel tips page, which also covers packing, traveling with kids, and traveling with pets.

Simple Tips for Arrival Day

  • Check your flight status before you leave home.
  • Keep your photo ID where you can reach it.
  • Never leave bags alone, even for a minute.
  • When you land, follow the signs for ground transportation, then head to the right door.
  • If you are being picked up, tell the driver exactly where you will wait. The airport curb is for quick stops, not long waits.

Conclusion

Ford Airport is busier every year, and the ride to and from it matters more than ever. Rideshare, taxis, the bus, hotel vans, and rental cars all have a place. A booked airport shuttle service in Grand Rapids can be a good fit when you have bags, a group, an early flight, or a long trip. Pick the choice that matches your day, plan your timing, and arrive calm.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find a rideshare at Ford Airport?

Leave the terminal through door 4, across from the Delta ticket counter. The Rideshare Shelter on the boulevard is the pickup and drop-off spot for all rideshare cars.

Is there a bus from the airport?

Yes. The Rapid runs Route 27 to the airport Monday through Friday. A ride costs $1.75. You catch it at the shelter across from door 6.

How early should I get to the airport?

The airport says to reach the ticket counter at least two hours before your flight. Airlines also want you at the gate at least 45 minutes before departure. Add more time if you travel with small children.

Do hotels near the airport offer free shuttles?

Many do. The airport lists a number of nearby hotels with courtesy shuttles. Check the information board in baggage claim, or call your hotel before you fly.

What details should I share when I book an airport shuttle?

Share your airline, flight number, number of people, number of bags, and your pickup or drop-off address. This helps match you with the right size of vehicle.
